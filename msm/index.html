About the Middle Square Method

The Middle Square Method is a classical pseudo-random number generation technique introduced by John von Neumann. It generates numbers by repeatedly squaring a seed and extracting the middle digits.

While it is not suitable for cryptographic security due to repetition and cycle issues, it is widely used for educational purposes in computer science and simulations.

⚠️ Not secure for encryption — only for learning and simulation
