Middle Square Method Algorithm

Step-by-step procedure for generating pseudo-random numbers

Algorithm:
  1. Start with a 4-digit seed number.
  2. Square the seed number.
  3. If the result has less than 8 digits, add leading zeros to make it 8 digits.
  4. Extract the middle 4 digits of the 8-digit number.
  5. Optionally extract the middle 2 digits for analysis.
  6. Use the middle 4 digits as the new seed.
  7. Repeat the process for the required number of iterations.
  8. Stop when:
    • A repeated value occurs (convergence)
    • The sequence becomes zero
    • Or the required iterations are completed
Convergence Condition:

The method is said to converge when the sequence starts repeating values, forming a loop, or becomes zero. At this point, no new random numbers are generated.
